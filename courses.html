7707 — O Level

O Level Accounting

Two papers, both on the whole syllabus, both with a calculator. Exams in the June and November series; grades A* to E.

What changes for 2027–2029

Students sitting in June or November 2026 follow the 2026 syllabus. From 2027:

  • Paper 1 grows40 questions in 1 hour 30 minutes, up from 35 in 1 hour 15. Still 30% of the grade.
  • Topic 7 is renamed“Accounting concepts and modern practice”, with new 7.2 Ethical considerations and 7.3 Technology and sustainability.
  • Ratios list updatedMark-up and inventory turnover in days join the list. Only the listed formulae are accepted.
  • Digital recordsThree-column running balances, digital ledgers, and the impact of digital transactions on bank reconciliations and control accounts.
  • Reordered and rewordedManufacturing becomes 5.4 and clubs 5.5; “allowance for irrecoverable debts” replaces “provision for doubtful debts”.
  • Paper 2 unchangedFive 20-mark questions, 1 hour 45 minutes, 70%.
