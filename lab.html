Work it out. Then check the working.

Ten tools for the calculations Cambridge papers test every series, plus a multiple-choice drill and flashcards. Each one lays out its working the way a mark scheme would, so you see where every figure comes from.

Tool 02 · 7707 4.2 · 9706 1.3

Depreciation & disposal

Build the schedule by straight-line, reducing balance or revaluation, compare the two main methods on one chart, then sell the asset and post the disposal account.

Tool 03 · 7707 3.3 · 9706 1.4

Bank reconciliation

First update the cash book for items only the bank knew about. Then reconcile the updated balance to the bank statement with the timing differences.

Tool 04 · 7707 6.1–6.3 · 9706 1.6, 3.5

Ratio analyser

Enter two years of figures. Every ratio is worked with Cambridge’s formula, compared year on year, and given a line of interpretation you can build an answer from.

Tool 05 · 9706 2.1

FIFO & AVCO

Run the same receipts and issues through FIFO, perpetual AVCO and periodic AVCO, and see how the choice changes closing inventory and profit.

Tool 06 · 9706 2.2

Break-even & CVP

Contribution, break-even point, C/S ratio, margin of safety and target profit. Drag across the chart to read profit or loss at any output: Cambridge asks you to interpret this chart, not draw it.

Tool 07 · 9706 4.2

Standard costing variances

Set the standard cost card and the actual results. Every variance in the syllabus is calculated, labelled favourable or adverse, and shown with its formula.

Tool 08 · 9706 4.4

Investment appraisal

Payback, accounting rate of return, net present value with three-decimal discount factors, and the internal rate of return by interpolation, as you would set it out in Paper 4.

Tool 09 · 7707 5.2 · 9706 1.5

Partnership appropriation

Share out a year’s profit: interest on drawings back in, salaries and interest on capital out, the residue in the profit-sharing ratio. Then write up each partner’s current account.
